    Floor 4: tactical level

    Grammar of the office

    Accountants write in the passive, managers rely on the right prepositions, and everyone needs the right word form.

    1. The passive voice

    be + past participle (V3). Use the passive when the action or result matters more than who did it. It's everywhere in reports and accounting.

    Add by + person only when it's important who did it: The sale was approved by the board.

    TenseFormExample
    Present simpleam / is / are + V3Transactions are recorded every day.
    Past simplewas / were + V3The payment was authorized yesterday.
    Present perfecthas / have been + V3The old warehouse has been sold.
    Futurewill be + V3A new supplier will be chosen next quarter.
    Modalmust / can / should be + V3All invoices must be checked.

    Choose the correct form

    Say it in the passive

    Say your answer out loud, then tap to check.

    2. Word + preposition

    These words always travel with the same preposition. Learn them as one chunk: rely on, concerned about, impact on, invest in, responsible for, allocate to, commitment to, compared with.

    3. Word formation

    Endings change the job of a word. -tion / -ation and -ment make nouns from verbs. -er / -ant make a person. -ity makes a noun from an adjective. -ly makes an adverb. in- makes the opposite.

    meticulousmeticulousnessmeticulously
